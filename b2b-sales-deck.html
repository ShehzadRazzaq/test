01 — Target Market & ICP

B2B Service Companies

Businesses that rely on trust, credibility, and lead generation to grow.

Industries

Logistics • Manufacturing • Wholesale Distribution • Consulting • Recruitment • IT Services • Engineering Services

Company Profile

Revenue: $500K – $10M+ • Employees: 10–100 • Age: 3–20 Years • Location: USA, Canada, UK, Australia, UAE • Decision Makers: Founder, CEO, Managing Director, Owner, Operations Director

Core Need: Generate more leads, build credibility, and improve operational efficiency.

02 — Why This Market

Strong Budgets, Clear ROI, and Accessible Decision Makers

✓ High Purchasing Power — Projects commonly range from $5,000–$20,000+.
✓ Clear ROI — Better websites, stronger brands, and automation directly impact revenue.
✓ Easy To Reach — Decision makers are accessible via LinkedIn, email, and company websites.
✓ Underserved Digitally — Many established businesses still rely on outdated sites and manual processes.

07 — Outreach Messaging Formula

Simple, Personalized, High-Relevance Structure

Observation — “I noticed...”
Problem — “This may be limiting...”
Opportunity — “We’ve helped businesses improve...”
CTA — “Would you be open to a few suggestions?”

Avoid: Long pitches, generic templates, and selling immediately.
